## 3. Governing Clinical Decisions (D10 – D15)

1. **D10 (Model Parsimony & Feature Scope):** Plain Logistic Regression selected over tree models. Features locked to age, sex, BMI, waist circumference, recent BP, hypertension history, and smoking.
2. **D11 (Temporal Split & Evaluation Targets):** Evaluated strictly on NHANES 2017–2023 temporal test split. AUROC 0.7527 exceeds $\ge 0.75$ bar.
3. **D12 (Provider-Only Risk Panel):** Risk panel displayed strictly to clinicians on provider endpoints (`/api/provider/review/{id}`). Patient leak defense strictly verified (patients never see probability or raw logits).
4. **D13 (Applicability Boundary):** Risk assessment is evaluated ONLY for patients with documented hypertension who do NOT have diagnosed diabetes. Patients with diabetes receive `null`.
5. **D14 (Can-Only-Raise Safety Invariant):** When `RISK_CAN_RAISE=true`, high risk band can only escalate `routine -> review`. It can never lower or override an urgent or emergency triage decision.
6. **D15 (Preventive Care Screening Reminders):** Configured dormant by default (`SCREENING_REMINDER_INTERVAL_DAYS=`) until clinicians configure interval in days.
