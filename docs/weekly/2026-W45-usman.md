## 4. Key Architectural & Safety Decisions

1. **Doctor-Approved Safety Rules (R1–R8, PR #106):**  
   Consensus from Dr. Shaiza Jabbar, Capt. Dr. Talha Yasin, and Dr. Anam Zafar codified into clinical rules: immediate meter LO/HI emergency escalation, neutral fast-acting sugar wording, AHA/ACC 2025 blood pressure citation, fluid-intolerance vomiting split, 365-day HbA1c screening reminder, provider-only risk panel, glucose unit clarification re-ask, and symptom-free no-readings routine triage.
2. **Interview Engine v3 NO-GO (PR #112):**  
   Based on benchmark data on 64 personas (4 v3 misses, 8 both-misses), v2 was retained as the default production interview engine (`INTERVIEW_ENGINE="v2"`), preventing untested conversational regressions.
3. **ADA 2026 Written Academic Permission (PR #118):**  
   Executed formal agreement authorizing 6 sections of *Standards of Care in Diabetes—2026*. Enforced Rule G3 (Gemini transmission strictly blocked; Groq only), mandatory citation/DOI formatting, and automated deletion procedure on 30 June 2027.
4. **Real-Corpus Explainer Hardening (PR #123, #125, #126):**  
   Diagnosed and resolved real-corpus failure modes: token truncation in Urdu JSON (`EXPLAIN_TRANSLATION_MAX_TOKENS=700`), diabetes chunk leakage into hypertension queries (pre-ranking prefix filtering), and Groq OTPM pacer join timeouts (extending join deadline against job deadline).
5. **Release 2 Language Gate (PR #130, #131):**  
   Human read on 8 Nov showed semantic drift in Urdu LLM translations. Implemented `EXPLAIN_LLM_LANGUAGES=en` to enforce the "0 wrong" release standard: English check-ins receive grounded LLM explanations, while Urdu and Roman Urdu patients receive verified deterministic templates.
