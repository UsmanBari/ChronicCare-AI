## 2. Academic Publishing & Presentation Rules

To maintain strict compliance with our licensing agreements, all team members, authors, and presenters must follow these rules across the FYP-1 report, academic papers, presentation slides, posters, and live demonstrations:

1. **No Raw Guideline Text:**
   - Do **NOT** paste, reproduce, or publish raw excerpt text from the ADA guidelines in papers, report appendices, slides, or posters. (Reproducing excerpts in published documents constitutes redistribution).
2. **Proper Reference Citations:**
   - Cite the ADA Standards of Care normally in bibliographies and academic reference lists.
3. **No Logo Use:**
   - The ADA logo and branding must **never** appear in the report, presentation slides, website UI, or promotional materials.
4. **Demonstration & Screenshot Guidelines:**
   - Screenshots of patient explanations in slides and reports must come from **hypertension** check-ins (e.g., WHO 2021 / ISH 2020) or display our own synthesized plain-language text.
   - Do not display raw ADA guideline chunk text in public demonstrations.
